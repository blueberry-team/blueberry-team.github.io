Lingding Account Deletion

How to delete your Lingding account and its data. Developer: BLUEBERRY STORE

Delete in the app

  1. Open the Lingding app and sign in.
  2. Go to the My tab and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account, read the notice, then tap Delete.

Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

If you cannot use the app

Email us with the account you signed up with (Google, Apple, Kakao, LINE or email) and your in-app nickname, with the subject "Account deletion request". We process requests within 7 business days.

Email: sonjungwoo9@gmail.com
