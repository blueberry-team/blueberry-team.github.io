Lingding Location-Based Service Terms

Effective date: November 6, 2026

한국어 · 日本語 · English

These terms are made under the Korean Act on the Protection, Use, etc. of Location Information. If the English and Korean versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

Article 1 (Purpose)

These terms set out the conditions for using the location-based service that BLUEBERRY STORE ("we") provides in the Lingding mobile app (the "Service"), and the rights and obligations of us and our users.

Article 2 (What the service does)

  • Region registration: if you agree, we measure your device's location and add your country and region (prefecture in Japan; province or metropolitan city in Korea) to your profile.
  • Region filter: when other users look for language exchange partners in a specific region, we match them using the registered region.

Location measurement and region registration are free of charge.

Article 3 (How and what we collect)

  • We measure your device's approximate location once, only when you have agreed to location collection and tap the location button. We never measure your location in the background.
  • The measured coordinates (latitude and longitude) are converted into a country and region on your device. The coordinates are never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Our servers store only a region code for your country and region (for example, JP-13).
  • Agreeing to location collection is optional. If you do not agree, or your location cannot be measured, you can sign up as an "Other region" user and use the Service.

When you withdraw consent or delete your account, we immediately delete the stored region code. We do not keep the measured coordinates on our servers.

Article 5 (Records of collection, use, and provision of location information)

  • Legal basis: Article 16(2) of the Act on the Protection, Use, etc. of Location Information; Article 6 of the Standards for Administrative and Technical Protection of Location Information
  • What we record: date and time of consent, collection, and withdrawal; region code; purpose of collection
  • Retention: 6 months from the date of the record, then deleted without delay.
  • If you withdraw consent or delete your account, we delete these records immediately, regardless of the retention period.

Article 7 (Rights of legal guardians)

The Service is only for users aged 14 or older (Article 3 of the Terms of Service). We do not collect personal location information from children under 14.

Article 8 (Third parties and disclosure)

  • Other users can see only your registered region (country and region), never your exact location.
