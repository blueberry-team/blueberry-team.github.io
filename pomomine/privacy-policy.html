Pomomine Privacy Policy

Effective: 16 September 2026

In short — no names or emails. The app sends only the device details and usage statistics it needs to run.

1. Kept on your device

What you dig up, your village stage and your focus records are stored only on your device. The developer never receives them. Deleting the app deletes them.

2. Sent to the developer's server

When the app is first opened, and once a day after that, it sends a random install ID created by the app (not linked to your name, phone number or advertising ID; it changes if you reinstall), the device type (Android/iOS), app version, OS version, device language, and the country code of your network. IP addresses are not stored. When you buy something, it also sends the store's purchase token or transaction ID, the product name and the price shown in the app. These are used only to count installs and daily use, and to confirm purchases with the store.

3. Usage statistics (Firebase Analytics)

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to see which features are used. In-app actions (starting or finishing a session, breaks, village stages) together with device type, OS, app version and approximate region are sent to Google. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Accounts

There is no sign-up and no login. The app never asks for your name, email or phone number.

5. Ads

The app shows Google AdMob rewarded ads, and only when you tap the button yourself. In serving ads, Google may process your advertising identifier and device information. See policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset the identifier or turn off personalised ads in your device settings.

6. Purchases

The triple-speed item is handled by Google Play or the App Store. Payment details never reach the app or the developer; the server only uses the purchase token or transaction ID from section 2 to check that the purchase exists. For refunds, see the refund guide.

7. Permissions

Notifications (to tell you a session ended), exact alarms (to be on time — optional), internet (ads, purchase checks and what is described in sections 2 and 3).

8. Children

This app is not directed at children.

9. Retention

Data sent to the server is kept while needed to run the service. Contact us to have it deleted.
