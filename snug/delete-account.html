Snug : Study Room アカウント削除のご案内

Snug : Study Room はログインなしで使えます。アプリが自動で作った匿名の識別子だけを用いており、氏名・メールアドレス・電話番号はお預かりしていません。

アプリから削除する

  1. アプリを開き、ホーム右上の SET（設定）を押します。
  2. いちばん下の アカウント削除 を押します。
  3. 確認画面で 削除する を押すと、下表の項目がその場で消去されます。

削除と同時に、参加中の部屋からも退出します。

本サービスは匿名で、メールアドレスとアカウントが結びついていません。そのためお問い合わせだけで特定するのは難しいのが実情です。ご連絡の際は、次の情報をできるだけ添えてください。

  • アプリに表示されていたニックネーム（例：しずかなペンギン）

Snug : Study Room — Deleting your account

Snug : Study Room works without any sign-in. It uses only an anonymous identifier the app generates for itself; we hold no name, email address or phone number.

Delete it from the app

  1. Open the app and tap SET (Settings) at the top right of the home screen.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom.
  3. Confirm with Delete. Everything in the table below is erased at that moment.

You are also removed from any room you are currently in.

What gets deleted

DataHandling
Anonymous account (token hash)Erased immediately
NicknameErased immediately
Total focus time and titleErased immediately
Focus records (time, minutes and room theme per cycle)Erased immediately
Token stored on your deviceErased immediately
Linked account (if any)Unlinked and erased immediately
Reports you filedKept up to 1 year so they can be reviewed, then destroyed
Note: this cannot be undone. Deleting also removes any linked Google or Apple account. The next time you open the app you start as a brand-new anonymous account.

If you already removed the app

Because the Service is anonymous, your email address is not linked to your account, so identifying it from an enquiry alone is difficult. Please include as much of the following as you can:

  • The nickname shown in the app (e.g. しずかなペンギン)
  • Roughly when you last used it
  • The device you used (iOS / Android)

Email: sonjungwoo9@gmail.com

Uninstalling the app alone leaves the record on the server. Deleting from inside the app before you uninstall is the reliable route.

Reporting someone

Use Report at the bottom of the room screen. Reporting immediately hides that person's content from you, and we review it within 24 hours.
